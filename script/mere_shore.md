## sign at 11,11

### text
- A stone marker on the western shore, and behind it the mouth of the cut -- where the mere should drain south toward Aldenmere.
- The channel is clear. The gate is open. The water stands against the opening and will not go through it.
- You put a hand in. It is cold, and it is moving: turning slowly, the whole mere turning, the way water turns around something going down.
- Nothing is going down. There is nothing under it but shingle, and whatever the shingle is lying on.
- Something is holding the Hollowmere, and holding it on purpose, and it is not finished.
