## the_dry_cut -- The Dry Cut
<!-- quest the_dry_cut -->

### name
- The Dry Cut

### summary
- Aldenmere's well is low and the channel that feeds it has been dry eleven days. The mere it drains has not dropped an inch.

### step rumour
- Ask in Aldenmere what is wrong with the water.

### step the_road
- Take the fen road north, toward the mere.

### step the_sluice
- Get past the sluice and reach the shore.

### step the_shore
- Find what the mere is holding.

### step the_answer
- Carry what you found back to Aldenmere.

## the_weight_of_the_lock -- The Weight of the Lock
<!-- quest the_weight_of_the_lock -->

### name
- The Weight of the Lock

### summary
- [PLACEHOLDER] The sluice gate on the fen road is sound in every part but one: the counterweight that turns it is gone.

### step way_down
- [PLACEHOLDER] Look around the works under the sluice.

### step the_gap
- [PLACEHOLDER] Find something the counterweight could be made from.

### step the_founder
- [PLACEHOLDER] Take the iron to the founder in Aldenmere.

### step hang_it
- [PLACEHOLDER] Hang the new weight in the cradle under the sluice.
